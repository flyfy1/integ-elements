How to pronounce Ytterbium

Element 70, symbol Yb, lanthanide. Chinese: 镱 (yì).

  • US Ytterbium: /ɪˈtɜːrbiəm/ i-TUR-bee-əm slow
  • UK Ytterbium: /ɪˈtɜːbiəm/ i-TUR-bee-əm slow

Example

Can you find ytterbium on the periodic table?

Watch out

YET-er-BEE-um The first 'y' is a vowel /ɪ/, not a 'y' consonant, and stress falls on the second syllable: ih-TUR-bee-um.

Did you know

Four elements, ytterbium, yttrium, terbium and erbium, are all named after one village.

Where the name comes from

Named after Ytterby, a village in Sweden

FAQ

How do you pronounce Ytterbium?

US /ɪˈtɜːrbiəm/, UK /ɪˈtɜːbiəm/, said i-TUR-bee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it YET-er-BEE-um?

No. The first 'y' is a vowel /ɪ/, not a 'y' consonant, and stress falls on the second syllable: ih-TUR-bee-um.

What is Ytterbium in Chinese?

镱 (yì). Symbol Yb, atomic number 70.
