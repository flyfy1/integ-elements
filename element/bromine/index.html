How to pronounce Bromine

Element 35, symbol Br, halogen. Chinese: 溴 (xiù).

  • US Bromine: /ˈbroʊmiːn/ BROH-meen slow
  • UK Bromine: /ˈbrəʊmiːn/ BROH-meen slow

Example

Our teacher showed us bromine, a red-brown liquid.

Watch out

BROH-mine The ending -ine here sounds like 'meen', not like the word 'mine'.

Did you know

Bromine is a liquid at standard conditions; the only other element that is liquid is mercury.

Where the name comes from

From Greek βρῶμος (brōmos), 'stench'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Bromine?

US /ˈbroʊmiːn/, UK /ˈbrəʊmiːn/, said BROH-meen (stress on the capital letters).

Is it BROH-mine?

No. The ending -ine here sounds like 'meen', not like the word 'mine'.

What is Bromine in Chinese?

溴 (xiù). Symbol Br, atomic number 35.
