How to pronounce Potassium

Element 19, symbol K, alkali metal. Chinese: 钾 (jiǎ).

  • US Potassium: /pəˈtæsiəm/ pə-TA-see-əm slow
  • UK Potassium: /pəˈtæsiəm/ pə-TA-see-əm slow

Example

Bananas are a good source of potassium.

Watch out

POT-ass-ee-um The stress falls on the second syllable: puh-TAS-ee-um.

KAY-lee-um The symbol K comes from Latin kalium, but the English name is potassium.

Did you know

Potassium is a metal so soft that you can easily cut it with a knife.

Where the name comes from

From English potash, 'pot ash': ash of burnt wood heated with water in a pot

FAQ

How do you pronounce Potassium?

/pəˈtæsiəm/ (US and UK), said pə-TA-see-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it POT-ass-ee-um?

No. The stress falls on the second syllable: puh-TAS-ee-um.

What is Potassium in Chinese?

钾 (jiǎ). Symbol K, atomic number 19.
