How to pronounce Niobium

Element 41, symbol Nb, transition metal. Chinese: 铌 (ní).

  • US Niobium: /naɪˈoʊbiəm/ ny-OH-bee-əm slow
  • UK Niobium: not verified slow

Example

Niobium makes the steel in gas pipelines stronger.

Watch out

nee-OH-bee-um The first 'i' is said like 'eye' (nye-OH-bee-um), not 'ee' as in pinyin 'ni'.

Did you know

Of 44,500 tonnes of niobium mined in 2006, an estimated 90% was used in high-grade structural steel.

Where the name comes from

From Greek myth: Niobe, daughter of Tantalus, the namesake of tantalum

FAQ

How do you pronounce Niobium?

US /naɪˈoʊbiəm/, said ny-OH-bee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it nee-OH-bee-um?

No. The first 'i' is said like 'eye' (nye-OH-bee-um), not 'ee' as in pinyin 'ni'.

What is Niobium in Chinese?

铌 (ní). Symbol Nb, atomic number 41.
