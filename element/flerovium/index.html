How to pronounce Flerovium

Element 114, symbol Fl, post-transition metal. Chinese: 𫓧 (fū).

Did you know

Flerovium is very volatile and may even be a gas at standard temperature and pressure.

Where the name comes from

Named after the Flerov Laboratory of Nuclear Reactions in Dubna, Russia
