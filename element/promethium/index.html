How to pronounce Promethium

Element 61, symbol Pm, lanthanide. Chinese: 钷 (pǒ).

  • US Promethium: /prəˈmiːθiəm/ prə-MEE-thee-əm slow
  • UK Promethium: not verified slow

Example

Promethium was once used in glowing paint for signal lights.

Watch out

pro-MEE-see-um The th is the tongue-between-teeth /θ/ sound, not s: pro-MEE-thee-um.

Did you know

At any moment, only about 500–600 grams of promethium exist naturally in all of Earth's crust.

Where the name comes from

From Greek myth: Prometheus, the Titan who stole fire and gave it to humans

FAQ

How do you pronounce Promethium?

US /prəˈmiːθiəm/, said prə-MEE-thee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it pro-MEE-see-um?

No. The th is the tongue-between-teeth /θ/ sound, not s: pro-MEE-thee-um.

What is Promethium in Chinese?

钷 (pǒ). Symbol Pm, atomic number 61.
