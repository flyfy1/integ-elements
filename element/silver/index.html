How to pronounce Silver

Element 47, symbol Ag, transition metal. Chinese: 银 (yín).

  • US Silver: /ˈsɪlvər/ SIL-vər slow
  • UK Silver: /ˈsɪlvə/ SIL-və slow

Example

She wore a silver necklace to the school party.

Did you know

In ancient Egypt, silver once cost more than gold.

Where the name comes from

From Old English seolfor (also siolfor), the word for this metal

FAQ

How do you pronounce Silver?

US /ˈsɪlvər/, UK /ˈsɪlvə/, said SIL-vər (stress on the capital letters).

What is Silver in Chinese?

银 (yín). Symbol Ag, atomic number 47.
