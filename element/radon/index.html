How to pronounce Radon

Element 86, symbol Rn, noble gas. Chinese: 氡 (dōng).

  • US Radon: /ˈreɪdɑːn/ RAY-dahn slow
  • UK Radon: /ˈreɪdɒn/ RAY-don slow

Example

We tested our basement for radon last winter.

Watch out

RAH-don The first 'a' sounds like 'ray', so say RAY-don, not the pinyin-style 'ra'.

Did you know

Radon is heavier than air, so it can collect in basements under people's homes.

FAQ

How do you pronounce Radon?

US /ˈreɪdɑːn/, UK /ˈreɪdɒn/, said RAY-dahn (stress on the capital letters).

Is it RAH-don?

No. The first 'a' sounds like 'ray', so say RAY-don, not the pinyin-style 'ra'.

What is Radon in Chinese?

氡 (dōng). Symbol Rn, atomic number 86.
