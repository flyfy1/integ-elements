How to pronounce Xenon

Element 54, symbol Xe, noble gas. Chinese: 氙 (xiān).

  • US Xenon: /ˈzenɑːn/ ZE-nahn slow
  • UK Xenon: /ˈzenɒn/ ZE-non slow

Example

Our camera flash uses xenon gas to make bright light.

Watch out

KSEE-non An x at the start of an English word is said /z/, so xenon begins like 'zee'.

Did you know

When frozen solid, xenon is denser than granite rock.

Where the name comes from

From Greek xénos, 'foreigner', 'stranger' or 'guest'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Xenon?

US /ˈzenɑːn/, UK /ˈzenɒn/, said ZE-nahn (stress on the capital letters).

Is it KSEE-non?

No. An x at the start of an English word is said /z/, so xenon begins like 'zee'.

What is Xenon in Chinese?

氙 (xiān). Symbol Xe, atomic number 54.
