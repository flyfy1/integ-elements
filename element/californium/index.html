How to pronounce Californium

Element 98, symbol Cf, actinide. Chinese: 锎 (kāi).

  • US Californium: /ˌkælɪˈfɔːrniəm/ ka-li-FAWR-nee-əm slow
  • UK Californium: /ˌkælɪˈfɔːniəm/ ka-li-FAW-nee-əm slow

Example

Our teacher said californium was named after a US state.

Did you know

Element 118, oganesson, was made by hitting californium-249 atoms with calcium-48 ions.

Where the name comes from

Named after the university and the U.S. state of California
