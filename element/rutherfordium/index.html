How to pronounce Rutherfordium

Element 104, symbol Rf, transition metal. Chinese: 𬬻 (lú).

  • US Rutherfordium: /ˌrʌðərˈfɔːrdiəm/ ru-dhər-FAWR-dee-əm slow
  • UK Rutherfordium: /ˌrʌðəˈfɔːdiəm/ ru-dhə-FAW-dee-əm slow

Example

Rutherfordium is too rare for any school lab to have.

Watch out

RU-ser-FOR-dee-um The th is a voiced /ð/ (tongue between teeth), not s or z; say ruh-ther-FOR-dee-um.

Did you know

Books in the Soviet Bloc once officially called element 104 "kurchatovium".

Where the name comes from

Named after New Zealand physicist Ernest Rutherford, the "father" of nuclear physics

FAQ

How do you pronounce Rutherfordium?

US /ˌrʌðərˈfɔːrdiəm/, UK /ˌrʌðəˈfɔːdiəm/, said ru-dhər-FAWR-dee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it RU-ser-FOR-dee-um?

No. The th is a voiced /ð/ (tongue between teeth), not s or z; say ruh-ther-FOR-dee-um.

What is Rutherfordium in Chinese?

𬬻 (lú). Symbol Rf, atomic number 104.
