How to pronounce Polonium

Element 84, symbol Po, metalloid. Chinese: 钋 (pō).

  • US Polonium: /pəˈloʊniəm/ pə-LOH-nee-əm slow
  • UK Polonium: /pəˈləʊniəm/ pə-LOH-nee-əm slow

Example

Marie Curie named polonium after her home country, Poland.

Did you know

Only about 100 grams of polonium are produced each year, practically all of it in Russia.

Where the name comes from

Named after Poland (Polonia), Marie Curie's native land

FAQ

How do you pronounce Polonium?

US /pəˈloʊniəm/, UK /pəˈləʊniəm/, said pə-LOH-nee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

What is Polonium in Chinese?

钋 (pō). Symbol Po, atomic number 84.
