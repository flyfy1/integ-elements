How to pronounce Berkelium

Element 97, symbol Bk, actinide. Chinese: 锫 (péi).

  • US Berkelium: /ˈbɜːrkliəm/ BUR-klee-əm slow
  • UK Berkelium: /bɜːˈkiːliəm/ bur-KEE-lee-əm slow

Example

Our chemistry teacher told us that berkelium is very rare.

Did you know

Just over one gram of berkelium has been produced in the United States since 1967.

Where the name comes from

From English place name Berkeley: named after the city of Berkeley, California

FAQ

How do you pronounce Berkelium?

US /ˈbɜːrkliəm/, UK /bɜːˈkiːliəm/, said BUR-klee-əm (stress on the capital letters).
