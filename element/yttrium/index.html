How to pronounce Yttrium

Element 39, symbol Y, transition metal. Chinese: 钇 (yǐ).

  • US Yttrium: /ˈɪtriəm/ I-tree-əm slow
  • UK Yttrium: /ˈɪtriəm/ I-tree-əm slow

Example

Yttrium helps the LED lights in our classroom glow.

Watch out

YIT-ree-um The first Y is a vowel, so the name starts with the sound /ɪ/ (IT-ree-um), with no 'y' sound in front.

Did you know

Until the early 1920s, yttrium's chemical symbol was Yt, not Y.

Where the name comes from

Named after the mineral ytterbite, which was named after the village of Ytterby in Sweden

FAQ

How do you pronounce Yttrium?

/ˈɪtriəm/ (US and UK), said I-tree-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it YIT-ree-um?

No. The first Y is a vowel, so the name starts with the sound /ɪ/ (IT-ree-um), with no 'y' sound in front.

What is Yttrium in Chinese?

钇 (yǐ). Symbol Y, atomic number 39.
