How to pronounce Neodymium

Element 60, symbol Nd, lanthanide. Chinese: 钕 (nǚ).

  • US Neodymium: /ˌniːoʊˈdɪmiəm/ nee-oh-DI-mee-əm slow
  • UK Neodymium: not verified slow

Example

My headphones use a tiny neodymium magnet.

Did you know

Glass containing neodymium can look like a different color under different kinds of light.

Where the name comes from

From Greek neos, 'new', + didymos, 'twin'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Neodymium?

What is Neodymium in Chinese?

钕 (nǚ). Symbol Nd, atomic number 60.
