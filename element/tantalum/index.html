How to pronounce Tantalum

Element 73, symbol Ta, transition metal. Chinese: 钽 (tǎn).

  • US Tantalum: /ˈtæntələm/ TAN-tə-ləm slow
  • UK Tantalum: /ˈtæntələm/ TAN-tə-ləm slow

Example

Some phones contain a little tantalum inside.

Did you know

Before tungsten took over, tantalum wires were used as light bulb filaments.

Where the name comes from

Named after Tantalus, a figure in Greek mythology

FAQ

How do you pronounce Tantalum?

/ˈtæntələm/ (US and UK), said TAN-tə-ləm (stress on the capital letters).

What is Tantalum in Chinese?

钽 (tǎn). Symbol Ta, atomic number 73.
