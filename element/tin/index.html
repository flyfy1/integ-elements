How to pronounce Tin

Element 50, symbol Sn, post-transition metal. Chinese: 锡 (xī).

  • US Tin: /tɪn/ TIN slow
  • UK Tin: /tɪn/ TIN slow

Example

We collected tin cans for the school recycling project.

Did you know

When you bend a bar of tin, it makes a sound called the 'tin cry'.

Where the name comes from

From Proto-Germanic *tin-om; German Zinn and Dutch tin share the same root

FAQ

How do you pronounce Tin?

/tɪn/ (US and UK), said TIN (stress on the capital letters).

What is Tin in Chinese?

锡 (xī). Symbol Sn, atomic number 50.
