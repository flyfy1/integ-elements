How to pronounce Iridium

Element 77, symbol Ir, transition metal. Chinese: 铱 (yī).

  • US Iridium: /ɪˈrɪdiəm/ i-RI-dee-əm slow
  • UK Iridium: /ɪˈrɪdiəm/ i-RI-dee-əm slow

Example

My dad's car uses iridium spark plugs that last a long time.

Watch out

EYE-rid-ee-um Because of the word 'iris', learners often start with 'eye', but iridium starts with a short 'i' and is stressed on the second syllable: ih-RID-ee-um.

Did you know

Unusually high iridium in a clay layer led to the idea that an impact ended the non-bird dinosaurs.

Where the name comes from

From Greek iris, 'rainbow', for the various colors of its compounds

FAQ

How do you pronounce Iridium?

/ɪˈrɪdiəm/ (US and UK), said i-RI-dee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it EYE-rid-ee-um?

No. Because of the word 'iris', learners often start with 'eye', but iridium starts with a short 'i' and is stressed on the second syllable: ih-RID-ee-um.

What is Iridium in Chinese?

铱 (yī). Symbol Ir, atomic number 77.
