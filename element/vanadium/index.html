How to pronounce Vanadium

Element 23, symbol V, transition metal. Chinese: 钒 (fán).

  • US Vanadium: /vəˈneɪdiəm/ və-NAY-dee-əm slow
  • UK Vanadium: /vəˈneɪdiəm/ və-NAY-dee-əm slow

Example

These strong tools are made with vanadium steel.

Watch out

va-NAH-dee-um The stressed 'a' in -na- is said like 'nay' (və-NAY-dee-əm), not 'nah', and the first 'a' is weak.

Did you know

Vanadium's first large-scale industrial use was in the steel alloy chassis of the Ford Model T.

Where the name comes from

From Old Norse Vanadís, another name for the goddess Freyja (beauty and fertility)

FAQ

How do you pronounce Vanadium?

/vəˈneɪdiəm/ (US and UK), said və-NAY-dee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it va-NAH-dee-um?

No. The stressed 'a' in -na- is said like 'nay' (və-NAY-dee-əm), not 'nah', and the first 'a' is weak.

What is Vanadium in Chinese?
