How to pronounce Cobalt

Element 27, symbol Co, transition metal. Chinese: 钴 (gǔ).

  • US Cobalt: /ˈkoʊbɔːlt/ KOH-bawlt slow
  • UK Cobalt: /ˈkəʊbɔːlt/ KOH-bawlt slow

Example

My phone battery contains a small amount of cobalt.

Did you know

People have used cobalt to color glass since the Bronze Age.

Where the name comes from

Possibly from German kobelt, the name 16th-century silver miners gave a nuisance type of ore

FAQ

How do you pronounce Cobalt?

US /ˈkoʊbɔːlt/, UK /ˈkəʊbɔːlt/, said KOH-bawlt (stress on the capital letters).

What is Cobalt in Chinese?

钴 (gǔ). Symbol Co, atomic number 27.
