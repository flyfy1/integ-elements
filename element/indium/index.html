How to pronounce Indium

Element 49, symbol In, post-transition metal. Chinese: 铟 (yīn).

  • US Indium: /ˈɪndiəm/ IN-dee-əm slow
  • UK Indium: /ˈɪndiəm/ IN-dee-əm slow

Example

Indium helps the touch screen on my phone work.

Did you know

One of indium's discoverers was color-blind, so his assistant had to spot the colored spectral lines.

Where the name comes from

From Latin indicum, 'of India', after the indigo color seen in its spectrum

FAQ

How do you pronounce Indium?

What is Indium in Chinese?
