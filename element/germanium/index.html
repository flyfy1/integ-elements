How to pronounce Germanium

Element 32, symbol Ge, metalloid. Chinese: 锗 (zhě).

  • US Germanium: /dʒɜːrˈmeɪniəm/ jur-MAY-nee-əm slow
  • UK Germanium: not verified slow

Example

Our teacher showed us a small piece of germanium.

Watch out

GER-man-ee-um The g is soft /dʒ/ as in 'Germany', and the stress falls on the second syllable: jer-MAY-nee-um.

Did you know

Like water, germanium expands as it freezes from a liquid, which few substances do.

Where the name comes from

From Latin Germania, 'Germany', in honor of Winkler's homeland

FAQ

How do you pronounce Germanium?

US /dʒɜːrˈmeɪniəm/, said jur-MAY-nee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it GER-man-ee-um?

No. The g is soft /dʒ/ as in 'Germany', and the stress falls on the second syllable: jer-MAY-nee-um.

What is Germanium in Chinese?

锗 (zhě). Symbol Ge, atomic number 32.
