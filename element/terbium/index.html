How to pronounce Terbium

Element 65, symbol Tb, lanthanide. Chinese: 铽 (tè).

  • US Terbium: /ˈtɜːrbiəm/ TUR-bee-əm slow
  • UK Terbium: not verified slow

Example

Terbium helps some lamps give off bright green light.

Did you know

Terbium has been found in the air of KELT-9b, a very hot planet outside our Solar System.

Where the name comes from

Named after Ytterby, a village in Sweden

FAQ

How do you pronounce Terbium?

US /ˈtɜːrbiəm/, said TUR-bee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

What is Terbium in Chinese?

铽 (tè). Symbol Tb, atomic number 65.
