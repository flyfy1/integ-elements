How to pronounce Curium

Element 96, symbol Cm, actinide. Chinese: 锔 (jū).

  • US Curium: /ˈkjʊriəm/ KYUU-ree-əm slow
  • UK Curium: /ˈkjʊəriəm/ KYOOR-ree-əm slow

Example

Our teacher said curium was named after Marie and Pierre Curie.

Did you know

A scientist first revealed curium's discovery on a children's radio quiz show.

Where the name comes from

Named after the scientists Marie and Pierre Curie, known for their research on radioactivity

FAQ

How do you pronounce Curium?

What is Curium in Chinese?

锔 (jū). Symbol Cm, atomic number 96.
