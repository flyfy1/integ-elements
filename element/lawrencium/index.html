How to pronounce Lawrencium

Element 103, symbol Lr, actinide. Chinese: 铹 (láo).

  • US Lawrencium: /lɔːˈrensiəm/ law-REN-see-əm slow
  • UK Lawrencium: not verified slow

Example

Our chemistry teacher said lawrencium is made in particle accelerators.

Watch out

LOR-en-SEE-um The name Lawrence is stressed on its first syllable, but lawrencium moves the stress to REN: law-REN-see-um.

Did you know

The first lawrencium atoms were reportedly made by hitting a three-milligram californium target with boron.

FAQ

How do you pronounce Lawrencium?

US /lɔːˈrensiəm/, said law-REN-see-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it LOR-en-SEE-um?

No. The name Lawrence is stressed on its first syllable, but lawrencium moves the stress to REN: law-REN-see-um.

What is Lawrencium in Chinese?

铹 (láo). Symbol Lr, atomic number 103.
