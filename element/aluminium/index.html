How to pronounce Aluminium / Aluminum

Element 13, symbol Al, post-transition metal. Chinese: 铝 (lǚ).

  • US Aluminum: /əˈluːmɪnəm/ ə-LOO-mi-nəm slow
  • UK Aluminium: /ˌæljəˈmɪniəm/ a-lyə-MI-nee-əm slow

Example

We wrapped the sandwiches in aluminum foil for the picnic.

Watch out

AL-yoo-MIN-um Learners mix the two versions: US is a-LOO-mi-num (4 syllables), UK is al-yoo-MIN-ee-um (5 syllables).

Did you know

It was once so rare and hard to make that it cost more than gold.

Where the name comes from

From Latin alumen, 'alum', the mineral it was first collected from

FAQ

How do you pronounce Aluminium / Aluminum?

US /əˈluːmɪnəm/, UK /ˌæljəˈmɪniəm/, said ə-LOO-mi-nəm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it AL-yoo-MIN-um?

No. Learners mix the two versions: US is a-LOO-mi-num (4 syllables), UK is al-yoo-MIN-ee-um (5 syllables).

What is Aluminium / Aluminum in Chinese?

铝 (lǚ). Symbol Al, atomic number 13.
