How to pronounce Zinc

Element 30, symbol Zn, transition metal. Chinese: 锌 (xīn).

  • US Zinc: /zɪŋk/ ZINGK slow
  • UK Zinc: /zɪŋk/ ZINGK slow

Example

Sunscreen with zinc helps protect your skin at the beach.

Did you know

After iron, zinc is the most common trace metal in the human body.

Where the name comes from

Probably from German Zinke, 'prong, tooth', named by Paracelsus

FAQ

How do you pronounce Zinc?

/zɪŋk/ (US and UK), said ZINGK (stress on the capital letters).

What is Zinc in Chinese?

锌 (xīn). Symbol Zn, atomic number 30.
