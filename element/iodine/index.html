How to pronounce Iodine

Element 53, symbol I, halogen. Chinese: 碘 (diǎn).

  • US Iodine: /ˈaɪədaɪn/ EYE-ə-dyn slow
  • UK Iodine: /ˈaɪədiːn/ EYE-ə-deen slow

Example

The nurse put some iodine on my cut knee.

Watch out

yo-DEEN It starts with the sound 'eye' and the stress is on it: EYE-uh-dine (US) or EYE-uh-deen (UK).

Did you know

Not getting enough iodine affects about two billion people around the world.

Where the name comes from

From Ancient Greek Ιώδης (iodes), meaning 'violet'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Iodine?

US /ˈaɪədaɪn/, UK /ˈaɪədiːn/, said EYE-ə-dyn (stress on the capital letters).

Is it yo-DEEN?

No. It starts with the sound 'eye' and the stress is on it: EYE-uh-dine (US) or EYE-uh-deen (UK).

What is Iodine in Chinese?

碘 (diǎn). Symbol I, atomic number 53.
