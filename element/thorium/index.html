How to pronounce Thorium

Element 90, symbol Th, actinide. Chinese: 钍 (tǔ).

  • US Thorium: /ˈθɔːriəm/ THAW-ree-əm slow
  • UK Thorium: /ˈθɔːriəm/ THAW-ree-əm slow

Example

Old camping lamps sometimes used thorium in their gas mantles.

Watch out

SOR-ee-um The 'th' is /θ/, made with the tongue between the teeth, not an 's' or 't' sound.

Did you know

Thorium is estimated to be over three times as abundant as uranium in the Earth's crust.

Where the name comes from

Named after Thor, the Norse god of thunder and war

FAQ

How do you pronounce Thorium?

/ˈθɔːriəm/ (US and UK), said THAW-ree-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it SOR-ee-um?

No. The 'th' is /θ/, made with the tongue between the teeth, not an 's' or 't' sound.

What is Thorium in Chinese?

钍 (tǔ). Symbol Th, atomic number 90.
