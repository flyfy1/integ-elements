How to pronounce Hydrogen

Element 1, symbol H, nonmetal. Chinese: 氢 (qīng).

  • US Hydrogen: /ˈhaɪdrədʒən/ HY-drə-jən slow
  • UK Hydrogen: /ˈhaɪdrədʒən/ HY-drə-jən slow

Example

Water is made of hydrogen and oxygen.

Did you know

Hydrogen makes up about 75% of all normal matter in the universe.

Where the name comes from

Named because it makes water when it burns.

FAQ

How do you pronounce Hydrogen?

/ˈhaɪdrədʒən/ (US and UK), said HY-drə-jən (stress on the capital letters).
