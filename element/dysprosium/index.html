How to pronounce Dysprosium

Element 66, symbol Dy, lanthanide. Chinese: 镝 (dī).

  • US Dysprosium: /dɪsˈproʊziəm/ dis-PROH-zee-əm slow
  • UK Dysprosium: not verified slow

Example

Our teacher said dysprosium helps make magnets for electric cars.

Watch out

dye-SPROH-see-um The y is a short 'i' as in 'dis', and the s in -sium sounds like /z/: dis-PROH-zee-um.

Did you know

No mineral has yet been found in which dysprosium is the main rare earth.

Where the name comes from

From Greek dysprositos, 'hard to get'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Dysprosium?

US /dɪsˈproʊziəm/, said dis-PROH-zee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it dye-SPROH-see-um?

No. The y is a short 'i' as in 'dis', and the s in -sium sounds like /z/: dis-PROH-zee-um.

What is Dysprosium in Chinese?

镝 (dī). Symbol Dy, atomic number 66.
