How to pronounce Iron

Element 26, symbol Fe, transition metal. Chinese: 铁 (tiě).

  • US Iron: /ˈaɪərn/ EYE-ərn slow
  • UK Iron: /ˈaɪən/ EYE-ən slow

Example

Spinach is a good source of iron.

Watch out

EYE-ron Iron is said EYE-ern (UK EYE-uhn); the o is not pronounced as a full vowel.

Did you know

By mass, iron is the most common element on Earth, making up much of Earth's core.

FAQ

How do you pronounce Iron?

US /ˈaɪərn/, UK /ˈaɪən/, said EYE-ərn (stress on the capital letters).

Is it EYE-ron?

No. Iron is said EYE-ern (UK EYE-uhn); the o is not pronounced as a full vowel.

What is Iron in Chinese?

铁 (tiě). Symbol Fe, atomic number 26.
