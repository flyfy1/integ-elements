How to pronounce Fermium

Element 100, symbol Fm, actinide. Chinese: 镄 (fèi).

  • US Fermium: /ˈfɜːrmiəm/ FUR-mee-əm slow
  • UK Fermium: /ˈfɜːmiəm/ FUR-mee-əm slow

Example

Fermium was named after a famous scientist, our teacher said.

Did you know

Fermium was discovered in 1952 in the debris of the first detonation test of a hydrogen bomb.

Where the name comes from

Named after Enrico Fermi, one of the pioneers of nuclear physics.

FAQ

How do you pronounce Fermium?

US /ˈfɜːrmiəm/, UK /ˈfɜːmiəm/, said FUR-mee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

What is Fermium in Chinese?

镄 (fèi). Symbol Fm, atomic number 100.
