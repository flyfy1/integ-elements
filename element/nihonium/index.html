How to pronounce Nihonium

Element 113, symbol Nh, post-transition metal. Chinese: 鿭 (nǐ).

  • US Nihonium: not verified slow
  • UK Nihonium: not verified slow

Example

Nihonium is element 113 on our classroom periodic table.

Did you know

Its most stable known form, nihonium-286, has a half-life of only about 10 seconds.

Where the name comes from

From Japanese: one of the two Japanese pronunciations of the name of Japan
