How to pronounce Protactinium

Element 91, symbol Pa, actinide. Chinese: 镤 (pú).

  • US Protactinium: /ˌproʊtækˈtɪniəm/ proh-tak-TI-nee-əm slow
  • UK Protactinium: /ˌprəʊtækˈtɪniəm/ proh-tak-TI-nee-əm slow

Example

Our teacher said protactinium is too rare to use outside labs.

Watch out

PRO-tak-ti-NEE-um The stress falls on the third syllable: pro-tak-TIN-ee-um.

Did you know

Protactinium is one of the rarest and most expensive elements found in nature.

Where the name comes from

From Greek prôtos, 'first, before', + actinium: the parent of actinium

FAQ

How do you pronounce Protactinium?

US /ˌproʊtækˈtɪniəm/, UK /ˌprəʊtækˈtɪniəm/, said proh-tak-TI-nee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it PRO-tak-ti-NEE-um?

No. The stress falls on the third syllable: pro-tak-TIN-ee-um.

What is Protactinium in Chinese?

镤 (pú). Symbol Pa, atomic number 91.
