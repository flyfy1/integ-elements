How to pronounce Boron

Element 5, symbol B, metalloid. Chinese: 硼 (péng).

  • US Boron: /ˈbɔːrɑːn/ BAW-rahn slow
  • UK Boron: /ˈbɔːrɒn/ BAW-ron slow

Example

Our teacher showed us a small piece of boron today.

Did you know

Crystalline boron is silvery to black and extremely hard: 9.3 on the Mohs scale.

Where the name comes from

Coined from borax, the mineral it was isolated from, by analogy with carbon

FAQ

How do you pronounce Boron?

US /ˈbɔːrɑːn/, UK /ˈbɔːrɒn/, said BAW-rahn (stress on the capital letters).

What is Boron in Chinese?

硼 (péng). Symbol B, atomic number 5.
