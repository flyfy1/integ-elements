How to pronounce Strontium

Element 38, symbol Sr, alkaline earth metal. Chinese: 锶 (sī).

  • US Strontium: /ˈstrɑːntiəm/ STRAHN-tee-əm slow
  • UK Strontium: /ˈstrɒntiəm/ STRON-tee-əm slow

Example

My chemistry teacher showed us strontium burning with a red flame.

Did you know

At the peak of TV tube production, up to 75% of US strontium went into TV screen glass.

Where the name comes from

Named after Strontian, a Scottish village where it was found in the ores of lead mines

FAQ

How do you pronounce Strontium?

US /ˈstrɑːntiəm/, UK /ˈstrɒntiəm/, said STRAHN-tee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

What is Strontium in Chinese?

锶 (sī). Symbol Sr, atomic number 38.
