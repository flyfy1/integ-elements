How to pronounce Ruthenium

Element 44, symbol Ru, transition metal. Chinese: 钌 (liǎo).

  • US Ruthenium: /ruːˈθiːniəm/ roo-THEE-nee-əm slow
  • UK Ruthenium: /ruːˈθiːniəm/ roo-THEE-nee-əm slow

Example

Our teacher said ruthenium makes some pen tips very hard.

Watch out

ROO-ten-ee-um The th is a soft /θ/ sound and the stress falls on the second syllable: roo-THEE-nee-um.

Did you know

Many fountain pen nibs are tipped with a ruthenium alloy.

Where the name comes from

From Latin Ruthenia, a name for Russia, chosen to honour Russia

FAQ

How do you pronounce Ruthenium?

/ruːˈθiːniəm/ (US and UK), said roo-THEE-nee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it ROO-ten-ee-um?

No. The th is a soft /θ/ sound and the stress falls on the second syllable: roo-THEE-nee-um.

What is Ruthenium in Chinese?

钌 (liǎo). Symbol Ru, atomic number 44.
