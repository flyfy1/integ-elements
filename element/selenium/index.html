How to pronounce Selenium

Element 34, symbol Se, nonmetal. Chinese: 硒 (xī).

  • US Selenium: /səˈliːniəm/ sə-LEE-nee-əm slow
  • UK Selenium: /səˈliːniəm/ sə-LEE-nee-əm slow

Example

Brazil nuts contain selenium, which our bodies need in small amounts.

Watch out

SEL-ih-nee-um The stress falls on the second syllable, which has a long 'ee' sound: suh-LEE-nee-um.

Did you know

In 1873, Willoughby Smith found that light changes how well grey selenium conducts electricity.

Where the name comes from

Berzelius named it after the Moon, because it was similar to tellurium, named for the Earth

FAQ

How do you pronounce Selenium?

/səˈliːniəm/ (US and UK), said sə-LEE-nee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it SEL-ih-nee-um?

No. The stress falls on the second syllable, which has a long 'ee' sound: suh-LEE-nee-um.

What is Selenium in Chinese?

硒 (xī). Symbol Se, atomic number 34.
