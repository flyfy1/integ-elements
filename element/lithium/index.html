How to pronounce Lithium

Element 3, symbol Li, alkali metal. Chinese: 锂 (lǐ).

  • US Lithium: /ˈlɪθiəm/ LI-thee-əm slow
  • UK Lithium: /ˈlɪθiəm/ LI-thee-əm slow

Example

My phone has a lithium battery inside.

Watch out

LEE-see-um The th is /θ/ (tongue between teeth), not /s/: LITH-ee-um.

Did you know

Batteries use more than three-quarters of all the lithium produced.

Where the name comes from

From Greek lithos, 'stone', because it was found in a solid mineral.

FAQ

How do you pronounce Lithium?

/ˈlɪθiəm/ (US and UK), said LI-thee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it LEE-see-um?

No. The th is /θ/ (tongue between teeth), not /s/: LITH-ee-um.

What is Lithium in Chinese?

锂 (lǐ). Symbol Li, atomic number 3.
