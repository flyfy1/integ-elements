How to pronounce Seaborgium

Element 106, symbol Sg, transition metal. Chinese: 𬭳 (xǐ).

  • US Seaborgium: not verified slow
  • UK Seaborgium: not verified slow

Example

Our teacher said seaborgium is named after a scientist.

Did you know

It is one of only two elements named after a living person at the time of naming; the other is oganesson.

Where the name comes from

Named after the American nuclear chemist Glenn T. Seaborg
