How to pronounce Silicon

Element 14, symbol Si, metalloid. Chinese: 硅 (guī).

  • US Silicon: /ˈsɪlɪkən/ SI-li-kən slow
  • UK Silicon: /ˈsɪlɪkən/ SI-li-kən slow

Example

The chip inside my phone is made from silicon.

Watch out

SIL-ih-KON The last syllable is weak, like 'kun' (SIL-ih-kun), not a full 'con'.

Did you know

Silicon makes up 27.2% of Earth's crust by weight, second only to oxygen.

FAQ

How do you pronounce Silicon?

/ˈsɪlɪkən/ (US and UK), said SI-li-kən (stress on the capital letters).

Is it SIL-ih-KON?

No. The last syllable is weak, like 'kun' (SIL-ih-kun), not a full 'con'.

What is Silicon in Chinese?

硅 (guī). Symbol Si, atomic number 14.
