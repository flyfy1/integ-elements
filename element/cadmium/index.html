How to pronounce Cadmium

Element 48, symbol Cd, transition metal. Chinese: 镉 (gé).

  • US Cadmium: /ˈkædmiəm/ KAD-mee-əm slow
  • UK Cadmium: /ˈkædmiəm/ KAD-mee-əm slow

Example

My teacher said cadmium is a toxic metal.

Did you know

In 2022, tests of 28 dark chocolate brands sold in the US found cadmium in all of them.

Where the name comes from

From Latin cadmia, Greek kadmeia, 'calamine', named after Cadmus, the mythical founder of Thebes

FAQ

How do you pronounce Cadmium?

/ˈkædmiəm/ (US and UK), said KAD-mee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

What is Cadmium in Chinese?

镉 (gé). Symbol Cd, atomic number 48.
