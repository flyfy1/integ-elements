How to pronounce Carbon

Element 6, symbol C, nonmetal. Chinese: 碳 (tàn).

  • US Carbon: /ˈkɑːrbən/ KAHR-bən slow
  • UK Carbon: /ˈkɑːbən/ KAH-bən slow

Example

Diamonds and pencil lead are both made of carbon.

Watch out

KAR-bon The second syllable is weak and sounds like 'bun' (KAR-bən), not a full 'bon'.

Did you know

In 1772, Antoine Lavoisier showed that diamonds are a form of carbon.

Where the name comes from

From Latin carbo, 'coal and charcoal'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Carbon?

US /ˈkɑːrbən/, UK /ˈkɑːbən/, said KAHR-bən (stress on the capital letters).

Is it KAR-bon?

No. The second syllable is weak and sounds like 'bun' (KAR-bən), not a full 'bon'.

What is Carbon in Chinese?

碳 (tàn). Symbol C, atomic number 6.
