How to pronounce Lead

Element 82, symbol Pb, post-transition metal. Chinese: 铅 (qiān).

  • US Lead: /led/ LED slow
  • UK Lead: /led/ LED slow

Example

My old fishing weights are made of lead.

Watch out

LEED The metal is said /led/ like 'red'; 'leed' is the verb meaning 'to guide'.

Did you know

Pencil 'leads' in wooden pencils have never been made from lead.

Where the name comes from

Of Germanic origin; comes from Middle English leed and Old English lēad

FAQ

How do you pronounce Lead?

/led/ (US and UK), said LED (stress on the capital letters).

Is it LEED?

No. The metal is said /led/ like 'red'; 'leed' is the verb meaning 'to guide'.

What is Lead in Chinese?

铅 (qiān). Symbol Pb, atomic number 82.
