How to pronounce Uranium

Element 92, symbol U, actinide. Chinese: 铀 (yóu).

  • US Uranium: /juˈreɪniəm/ yoo-RAY-nee-əm slow
  • UK Uranium: /juˈreɪniəm/ yoo-RAY-nee-əm slow

Example

Some power stations use uranium to make electricity.

Watch out

yoo-RAH-nee-um The stressed 'a' sounds like 'ay' in 'rain' (yoo-RAY-nee-um), not like pinyin 'ra'.

Did you know

Uranium is about 40 times more common in Earth's crust than silver.

Where the name comes from

Named by Klaproth after Uranus, a planet that had only recently been discovered

FAQ

How do you pronounce Uranium?

/juˈreɪniəm/ (US and UK), said yoo-RAY-nee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it yoo-RAH-nee-um?

No. The stressed 'a' sounds like 'ay' in 'rain' (yoo-RAY-nee-um), not like pinyin 'ra'.

What is Uranium in Chinese?

铀 (yóu). Symbol U, atomic number 92.
