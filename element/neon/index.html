How to pronounce Neon

Element 10, symbol Ne, noble gas. Chinese: 氖 (nǎi).

  • US Neon: /ˈniːɑːn/ NEE-ahn slow
  • UK Neon: /ˈniːɒn/ NEE-on slow

Example

The shop sign glows with bright red neon at night.

Did you know

A balloon filled with neon will rise in air, but more slowly than a helium balloon.

Where the name comes from

From Greek neon (νέον), a form of neos, meaning 'new'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Neon?

US /ˈniːɑːn/, UK /ˈniːɒn/, said NEE-ahn (stress on the capital letters).

What is Neon in Chinese?

氖 (nǎi). Symbol Ne, atomic number 10.
