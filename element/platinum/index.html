How to pronounce Platinum

Element 78, symbol Pt, transition metal. Chinese: 铂 (bó).

  • US Platinum: /ˈplætɪnəm/ PLA-ti-nəm slow
  • UK Platinum: /ˈplætɪnəm/ PLA-ti-nəm slow

Example

Her wedding ring is made of platinum.

Watch out

pluh-TEE-num The stress goes on the first syllable, PLAT-in-um, and the middle i is short and weak.

Did you know

One rock layer, the Merensky Reef, holds around 75% of the world's known platinum.

Where the name comes from

From Spanish platina, a diminutive of "silver" ('little silver')

FAQ

How do you pronounce Platinum?
