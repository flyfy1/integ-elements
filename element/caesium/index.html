How to pronounce Caesium / Cesium

Element 55, symbol Cs, alkali metal. Chinese: 铯 (sè).

  • US Cesium: /ˈsiːziəm/ SEE-zee-əm slow
  • UK Caesium: /ˈsiːziəm/ SEE-zee-əm slow

Example

Atomic clocks use cesium to keep very exact time.

Watch out

KAI-see-um The 'ae' is said like 'ee', the c is /s/ and the s is /z/: SEE-zee-um.

Did you know

Since 1967, the official length of one second has been defined using caesium atoms.

Where the name comes from

From Latin caesius, chosen for the bright blue lines in its spectrum

FAQ

How do you pronounce Caesium / Cesium?

/ˈsiːziəm/ (US and UK), said SEE-zee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it KAI-see-um?

No. The 'ae' is said like 'ee', the c is /s/ and the s is /z/: SEE-zee-um.

What is Caesium / Cesium in Chinese?

铯 (sè). Symbol Cs, atomic number 55.
