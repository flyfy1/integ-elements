How to pronounce Rhodium

Element 45, symbol Rh, transition metal. Chinese: 铑 (lǎo).

  • US Rhodium: /ˈroʊdiəm/ ROH-dee-əm slow
  • UK Rhodium: not verified slow

Example

Her white gold ring has a shiny rhodium coating.

Did you know

Tiny traces of rhodium have been measured in some potatoes.

Where the name comes from

From Greek rhodon, meaning 'rose'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Rhodium?
