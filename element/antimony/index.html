How to pronounce Antimony

Element 51, symbol Sb, metalloid. Chinese: 锑 (tī).

  • US Antimony: /ˈæntɪmoʊni/ AN-ti-moh-nee slow
  • UK Antimony: /ˈæntɪməni/ AN-ti-mə-nee slow

Example

Old car batteries contain lead mixed with antimony.

Watch out

an-TIM-uh-nee The stress falls on the first syllable, AN-ti-moh-nee, not the second.

Did you know

An artifact said to be part of a vase made of antimony, from about 3000 BC, was found in present-day Iraq.

Where the name comes from

From medieval Latin antimonium; where that word comes from is uncertain

FAQ

How do you pronounce Antimony?

US /ˈæntɪmoʊni/, UK /ˈæntɪməni/, said AN-ti-moh-nee (stress on the capital letters).

Is it an-TIM-uh-nee?

No. The stress falls on the first syllable, AN-ti-moh-nee, not the second.

What is Antimony in Chinese?

锑 (tī). Symbol Sb, atomic number 51.
