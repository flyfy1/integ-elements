How to pronounce Zirconium

Element 40, symbol Zr, transition metal. Chinese: 锆 (gào).

  • US Zirconium: /zɜːrˈkoʊniəm/ zur-KOH-nee-əm slow
  • UK Zirconium: not verified slow

Example

The dentist used a zirconium crown on my back tooth.

Did you know

Zirconium alloys wrap nuclear fuel rods because they absorb very few neutrons.

Where the name comes from

From the mineral zircon, related to Persian zargun, 'gold-like'

FAQ
