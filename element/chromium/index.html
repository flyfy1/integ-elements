How to pronounce Chromium

Element 24, symbol Cr, transition metal. Chinese: 铬 (gè).

  • US Chromium: /ˈkroʊmiəm/ KROH-mee-əm slow
  • UK Chromium: /ˈkrəʊmiəm/ KROH-mee-əm slow

Example

The chromium on my bike shines like a mirror.

Did you know

Polished chromium reflects almost 70% of visible light and almost 90% of infrared light.

Where the name comes from

From Greek chrōma, 'colour', because many of its compounds are brightly coloured

FAQ

How do you pronounce Chromium?

US /ˈkroʊmiəm/, UK /ˈkrəʊmiəm/, said KROH-mee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

What is Chromium in Chinese?
