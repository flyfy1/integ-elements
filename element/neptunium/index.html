How to pronounce Neptunium

Element 93, symbol Np, actinide. Chinese: 镎 (ná).

  • US Neptunium: /nepˈtuːniəm/ nep-TOO-nee-əm slow
  • UK Neptunium: /nepˈtjuːniəm/ nep-TYOO-nee-əm slow

Example

Our chemistry teacher said neptunium was first made in 1940.

Watch out

NEP-tyoo-nee-um The stress falls on the second syllable, nep-TOO-nee-um, not on the first syllable as in the planet name Neptune.

Did you know

Neptunium is used to make plutonium-238, which is used to provide electricity for spacecraft.

Where the name comes from

Named after Neptune, the planet beyond Uranus in the Solar System; uranium is named after Uranus.

FAQ

How do you pronounce Neptunium?

US /nepˈtuːniəm/, UK /nepˈtjuːniəm/, said nep-TOO-nee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it NEP-tyoo-nee-um?

No. The stress falls on the second syllable, nep-TOO-nee-um, not on the first syllable as in the planet name Neptune.

What is Neptunium in Chinese?

镎 (ná). Symbol Np, atomic number 93.
