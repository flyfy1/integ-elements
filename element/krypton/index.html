How to pronounce Krypton

Element 36, symbol Kr, noble gas. Chinese: 氪 (kè).

  • US Krypton: /ˈkrɪptɑːn/ KRIP-tahn slow
  • UK Krypton: /ˈkrɪptɒn/ KRIP-ton slow

Example

Some camera flashes use krypton to make bright white light.

Did you know

From 1960 to 1983, the official length of a metre was based on light from krypton.

FAQ
