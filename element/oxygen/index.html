How to pronounce Oxygen

Element 8, symbol O, nonmetal. Chinese: 氧 (yǎng).

  • US Oxygen: /ˈɑːksɪdʒən/ AHK-si-jən slow
  • UK Oxygen: /ˈɒksɪdʒən/ OK-si-jən slow

Example

Plants give off oxygen when sunlight shines on their leaves.

Did you know

Oxygen makes up 88.8% of the mass of the world's oceans.

Where the name comes from

From Greek oxys, 'sharp, acid', + -genes, 'producer'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Oxygen?
