How to pronounce Sodium

Element 11, symbol Na, alkali metal. Chinese: 钠 (nà).

  • US Sodium: /ˈsoʊdiəm/ SOH-dee-əm slow
  • UK Sodium: /ˈsəʊdiəm/ SOH-dee-əm slow

Example

This soup has too much sodium for my diet.

Watch out

NAT-ree-um The symbol Na comes from the Latin name natrium, but in English the element is called SOH-dee-um.

Did you know

Seawater holds an estimated 10.8 grams of sodium in every liter.

Where the name comes from

Thought to come from Arabic suda, meaning 'headache'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Sodium?

US /ˈsoʊdiəm/, UK /ˈsəʊdiəm/, said SOH-dee-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it NAT-ree-um?

No. The symbol Na comes from the Latin name natrium, but in English the element is called SOH-dee-um.

What is Sodium in Chinese?

钠 (nà). Symbol Na, atomic number 11.
