How to pronounce Barium

Element 56, symbol Ba, alkaline earth metal. Chinese: 钡 (bèi).

  • US Barium: /ˈberiəm/ BE-ree-əm slow
  • UK Barium: /ˈbeəriəm/ BAIR-ree-əm slow

Example

The doctor gave me a barium drink before my X-ray.

Watch out

BAH-ree-um The first 'a' sounds like the 'air' in 'hair' (BAIR-ee-um), not the open 'ah' in 'car'.

Did you know

Barium compounds are added to fireworks to give them a green colour.

Where the name comes from

From the alchemical word "baryta", from Greek βαρὺς, meaning 'heavy'

FAQ

How do you pronounce Barium?

US /ˈberiəm/, UK /ˈbeəriəm/, said BE-ree-əm (stress on the capital letters).

Is it BAH-ree-um?

No. The first 'a' sounds like the 'air' in 'hair' (BAIR-ee-um), not the open 'ah' in 'car'.

What is Barium in Chinese?

钡 (bèi). Symbol Ba, atomic number 56.
