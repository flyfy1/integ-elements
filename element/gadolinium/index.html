How to pronounce Gadolinium

Element 64, symbol Gd, lanthanide. Chinese: 钆 (gá).

  • US Gadolinium: /ˌgædəˈlɪniəm/ ga-də-LI-nee-əm slow
  • UK Gadolinium: /ˌgædəˈlɪniəm/ ga-də-LI-nee-əm slow

Example

Doctors sometimes use gadolinium to make MRI scans clearer.

Did you know

Below about 20 °C, gadolinium becomes magnetic and is pulled by magnets more strongly than nickel.

Where the name comes from

From the mineral gadolinite, itself named after the Finnish chemist Johan Gadolin
