How do you pronounce the names of the chemical elements?

Tap any element on the table to hear its name in American and British English, at normal and slow speed, with an example sentence. IPA comes from dictionaries and is shown only when two sources agree.

Why are there two spellings of aluminium and caesium?

Aluminium and caesium are the IUPAC and British spellings; aluminum and cesium are American. The site has both spellings and both accents.
